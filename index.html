air-quality-pipeline

github.com/chtanzeel/air-quality-pipeline

Hourly air quality data for Lahore, Faisalabad, Islamabad, Peshawar and Karachi, pulled from a public API every day and loaded incrementally into Postgres using watermarks. Modelled and tested with dbt, scheduled by Airflow, and started with one docker compose up.

  • Faisalabad has overtaken Lahore. Its smog-season mean PM2.5 rose three seasons running, from 116.3 to 136.2 µg/m³, while Lahore stayed around 110.
  • Lahore's worst week was 13 to 19 January 2026, with a 7-day mean of 219 µg/m³, about 15 times the WHO 24-hour guideline.
  • The checks caught an upstream outage. For 60 hours in December 2022 all five cities read near zero at once. Those rows are quarantined, and a dbt test now fails the build if an unreviewed outage like it appears again.

flight-lakehouse

github.com/chtanzeel/flight-lakehouse

A medallion pipeline on PySpark and Delta Lake. 3 million public flight records move through bronze, silver and gold tables. Bad rows are quarantined with the exact rule they broke, not dropped, a reconciliation gate stops silent data loss, and the tests run in CI on both Parquet and Delta.
